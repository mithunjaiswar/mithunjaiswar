Install

  1. Tap Download APK and open the file.
  2. If Android blocks it, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then open it again.
  3. Open the app, enter the server address above, your email and password (from your admin).
  4. Allow Call log and Phone permissions.
  5. Pick Today or a date range and tap Upload to server.
