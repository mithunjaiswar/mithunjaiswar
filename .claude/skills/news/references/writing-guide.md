# Writing Guide — TL;DR, Key Insights, Focus Areas

House voice: factual, compact, numbers-first. No hype, no filler. Every claim traceable to a cell.

## TL;DR — exactly 4 bullets

| Bullet | Content |
|---|---|
| **Snapshot:** | The 3–4 headline KPI moves with WoW, plus the direction story (streak, reversal, off-a-high). |
| **Highlights:** | The 2–3 genuinely good moves, city and product. |
| **Watch:** | The worst city/product, and anything moving the wrong way. |
| **Deposit Health:** | Healthy (>₹10K) share, ≤₹5K share, high-risk (≤₹2K) share, ₹20K+ slab. |

## Key Insights — 4–5 bullets per section

Pattern: `<entity> <verb> to <value> (<WoW> WoW) — <why it matters>.`
Colour-code the headline number in each bullet to match its trend direction.

Cover, in roughly this order: biggest gainer, current leader, the laggard/largest drag, biggest
decliner, then a one-line roll-up of the Overall figure. Do not just narrate the table — say what
the movement *means* (e.g. a recovery gain confirmed by the W/O-CtoR line is real cash, not deposit
conversion).

## Language discipline

- **Streaks:** count the actual consecutive moves. 75 → 75 → 76 → 77 is a *second* straight gain, not a third.
- **Extremes:** "four-week high" only if strictly beyond all four shown values. Never claim more weeks than are displayed.
- **Volatile series:** when a move is huge on a small or erratic base (EV bad debt, Revenue Share), say so — "should not be read as a trend yet". Do not let a -14pp swing read as an operational win.
- **Ratios:** "~1.7× the national rate" beats repeating both numbers.
- **Rounding tension:** if the source WoW says `0%` but the displayed values differ by 1, do not write "flat (0% WoW)" next to values that visibly moved — phrase around it.
- Avoid: "significant", "sharp", "dramatic", "concerning" without a number attached.

## Next Week Focus Areas — 5–6 bullets

Each is `**<Action label>:** <current state with numbers> <who owns the gap>`. Action labels are
imperative themes — Contain BD, Hold the Recovery Gain, Vehicle Recovery, Deposit Upgrade,
Advance Collections, Carry Forward Watch. Name the specific city or product, never "some cities".

## New Initiatives

**One line per initiative.** Format: `**<Label>:** <one plain sentence saying what it is>`.

Keep it to a single sentence. Do NOT pad with the metric it targets, the city breakdown, or a
rationale clause — that reads as filler next to the data sections, which carry the numbers already.

**Never invent status, owner, go-live date, or progress.** "Pilot scoping in progress", "back-tested",
"designs created" are only permitted when the user states them.

**Never infer who an initiative targets.** A "rental discount to existing drivers" is not
self-evidently a retention or win-back lever — it rewards partners who pay daily rental through the
full week. Ask the user rather than guessing the mechanism; a wrong target reads as a real error to
the city leads who run it.

Example: `**Vendor — vehicle recovery:** External vendors to speed up vehicle repossession.`
